# Vendora

**Offline-first, AI-ready shop management for Android.**

Vendora is being developed to help small businesses manage shop-specific products, stock, sales, and day-to-day operations from a mobile device.

<div class="grid cards" markdown>

-   :material-cellphone: **Mobile-first product**

    Designed around short, practical workflows on a phone.

-   :material-database-outline: **Offline-first foundation**

    Core business data should remain available without a network connection.

-   :material-store-outline: **Multiple shops**

    Keep each business's inventory and activity scoped to its own shop.

-   :material-image-outline: **Visual product direction**

    Review the [UI concepts](product/ui-concepts.md) before implementation.

</div>
