# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Vendora aims to be a respectful, practical space for people to build and improve software together. Participants are expected to communicate professionally and make collaboration safe and constructive regardless of experience level, identity, background, or personal circumstances.

This Code of Conduct applies to project spaces, including issues, pull requests, code reviews, discussions, and other project-related interactions.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and considerate.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Explain disagreements with evidence and technical reasoning.
- Accept good-faith corrections and feedback.
- Respect privacy; do not publish private information, credentials, or customer data.
- Keep discussions relevant and avoid harassment, insults, threats, discriminatory language, and unwanted personal contact.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, intimidation, doxxing, threats, deliberate disruption, discriminatory conduct, and publishing another person's private information without permission are not acceptable. Sexualized language or imagery is not appropriate in project spaces.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through a private GitHub channel. Do not use a public issue to report sensitive personal information. Include relevant links or evidence where safe.

The maintainer will review reports in good faith and may edit or remove content, close discussions, restrict participation, or block accounts when appropriate. The project currently has no separate moderation team or guaranteed response-time SLA.

## Scope and attribution

This policy applies within project spaces and when someone is officially representing the project.
